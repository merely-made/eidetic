# impresa

The association organ: what a pointable thing (a glyph, a creature, a
character, a faction, a place, an item) has come to be associated with, and
what has lapsed, over time. An impresa is a personal emblem with its motto.

The record type and its validation only. A product stores the records in its
own accepted history and derives its readings (fact, belief, legend) as
projections over what it stored. Kinds of association are a closed,
extensible set, seeded from `datasheets/kinds.toml`.
