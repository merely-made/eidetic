The supplied `launch.sh` SHA-256 is `932e1f6dea953374495c126433f2ec287c5327bd6079a504e38cc88512ccde30`. It executes `flock -w 60` around the approved collector and the named O output. The existing phase units are independently created in `app.slice` with their own 3 GiB, zero-swap, 150% CPU and 600-second bounds. The proposed controller's 512 MiB and 50% CPU bounds therefore apply to the controller, not those separate phase scopes. The controller log is outside the collection tree.

**Interruption boundary:** 1320 seconds covers the stated lock wait and two 610-second launcher waits plus 40 seconds, but source inventories, copying, aggregation and launcher cleanup also take time outside those waits. It is a deadline, not a proven completion bound. If the service times out or is stopped while a phase scope remains live, flock and the controller can exit before that independently managed scope empties. Its 600-second backstop remains, but the lock may be released and the normal cleanup receipt may be missing. Do not treat that run as collected; establish that its actual owned phase unit is inactive and empty before any further heavy work. Do not manually stop a running controller to finalize it.

On ordinary completion, retain the same controller InvocationID and exit status with its log, and require the collector's own per-phase ownership and empty-scope cleanup receipts and completed ledger. Stop only the matched service after it has finished. This review does not establish the cause of the earlier interruption, run the launcher, or qualify native behavior.
